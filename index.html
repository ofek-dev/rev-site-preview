FAQ

Questions people ask before hiring a VA

What does a real estate virtual assistant do?
A real estate virtual assistant handles the work that does not need a license: cold calling and appointment setting, transaction coordination, listing and MLS input, CRM follow-up, property management admin and marketing. Real Estate Virtual Staff places dedicated VAs for each of those roles.
How much does a real estate virtual assistant cost?
Between $7 and $11 per hour, depending on the candidate's experience, and that hourly rate is all you pay. Real Estate Virtual Staff searches, vets and introduces candidates free of charge, there is no placement fee, and there are no hidden costs. You see each candidate's exact hourly rate on their profile before you decide.
Is there any fee to search for or interview candidates?
No. The search, the vetting and the introductions to two or three candidates are free, with no commitment. You pay only the VA's hourly rate once you hire someone, and nothing if you do not.
Where are your virtual assistants based?
Across the Philippines, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia and other English-fluent markets. Every candidate, wherever they live, clears the same five-plus interview rounds and works the hours you set in your time zone.
Can a real estate VA work in my time zone?
Yes. Every VA we place works the hours you set, in your local time zone. We have placed VAs on Pacific, Mountain, Central and Eastern schedules, including evenings and weekends for lead response.
How are candidates vetted?
We only present the top 1% of applicants. Each candidate goes through five or more rounds: a written test, a live Zoom interview to verify English fluency, a general IQ test, role-specific skills and tools checks, and reference verification. You then see intro videos and resumes before any interview.
How fast can I hire a real estate virtual assistant?
You receive two or three vetted candidates with intro videos, resumes and rates within two weeks of your brief. Most clients interview and choose within a few days after that, and the VA can usually start the following week.
What if the VA is not a good fit?
We replace them at no additional charge, at any point, for as long as you work with us. That includes a VA who leaves. In practice it works like an unlimited trial: you pay the VA for hours worked, and you never pay us again to find a replacement.
Who employs and pays the virtual assistant?
You do. Real Estate Virtual Staff, a Talent Trooper company, recruits, vets and places the VA free of charge, prepares the agreement, and helps you set up compliant payment. You pay the VA's hourly rate only, and the VA works directly for your business like any team member.
Is an overseas VA better than hiring locally?
For admin, follow-up, coordination and marketing, most of our clients say yes: experienced, fluent, full-time help at $7 to $11 an hour instead of a local salary, with a replacement guarantee. Read our in-house vs. virtual assistant comparison for the full breakdown.
