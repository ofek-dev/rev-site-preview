How to Onboard a Real Estate VA in the First 30 Days

By Real Estate Virtual Staff · August 25, 2026

Before day one

  • Create role-based logins for your CRM, transaction system, MLS access where allowed, email and calendar
  • Set up a password manager and share credentials through it, never by text
  • Write a one-page brief: the role, the hours in your time zone, the tools, who they report to
  • Record three short screen videos of how you do the main tasks today

Week 1: access, tools and shadowing

Spend the first week making sure every login works and your VA can see how you work. A fifteen-minute video call each morning is enough. Have them shadow your CRM, read your templates and scripts, and complete small supervised tasks: tagging contacts, drafting a listing description, building a transaction timeline from a real contract.

Week 2: owned tasks with review

Hand over the repeatable work and review it daily: new-lead responses, showing coordination, document routing or social posts, depending on the role. Give feedback in writing in your shared channel so there is a record to refer back to. Expect questions; a VA who asks is doing the job right.

Week 3: autonomy with reporting

Move to a short daily written report instead of a call: what was done, what is waiting on you, what is next. For ISAs, that is conversations and appointments. For coordinators, open files and next deadlines. For marketing, posts scheduled and engagement.

Week 4: measure and expand

At day 30, review the numbers that matter for the role: response time to new leads, appointments set, deadlines met, listings live within a day of photos, posts published on schedule. Then add the next layer of tasks. Most clients find their VA has capacity for more by the end of the first month.

Common mistakes

  • Starting without logins ready, which wastes the first week
  • Explaining a task once verbally with no recording or written process
  • Skipping the daily check-in in week one and then being surprised by week three
  • Measuring activity instead of outcomes

If a placement is not working despite good onboarding, say so early. Every Real Estate Virtual Staff placement includes a replacement guarantee at no additional charge, and our account team helps diagnose whether the issue is the fit or the process.
