Property management is a volume business built on small, urgent tasks. A property management virtual assistant absorbs that volume: logging maintenance requests, dispatching vendors, answering tenant questions, processing applications and sending rent reminders, all inside your management software.

We shortlist VAs who have worked in AppFolio, Buildium or similar systems and can handle a frustrated tenant with patience and a clear process. Phone and written English are both verified, because this role talks to tenants, owners and vendors every day.

Your VA also prepares renewals, screens applications against your criteria, keeps vendor records current and builds the weekly owner-facing reports, so your managers see a clean dashboard instead of a backlog.

At about $7 to $11 an hour with no placement fee, one VA can cover a portfolio's admin that would otherwise need a full-time hire. Tell us your door count and your software, and we will send candidates within two weeks, free of charge.

FAQ

Questions about this role

Can a VA answer tenant calls, not just emails?
Yes. Many clients route a tenant line to their VA during scheduled hours for maintenance intake and general questions, with emergencies escalated by your rules.
Which property management software do your VAs use?
AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, Propertyware and TenantCloud are the most common. Say which you use and we match for it.
Can the VA coordinate vendors and maintenance?
Yes. Intake, vendor scheduling, follow-up until the ticket is closed and tenant confirmation are core to the role.
Can one VA support multiple property managers?
Yes. A full-time VA typically supports a small team of managers, handling the shared inbox, tickets and reporting.
