Winning the listing is the part you enjoy. Getting it live, photographed, shown and reported on is hours of admin per property. A listing and MLS admin virtual assistant does that work for you, so the listing goes live the day the photos come back and the seller hears from your office every week.

We shortlist VAs who have entered listings before and can write a clean, accurate description from your notes and photos. We test attention to detail, because a wrong square footage or a missed disclosure is not something you want to hear about from a buyer's agent.

Your listing VA books the photographer, the sign and the lockbox, coordinates showings through ShowingTime or whatever you use, collects feedback after every showing and turns it into a seller update. Price changes and status updates go out the same day you decide them.

Your VA works in your systems on your U.S. hours, so the admin gets done while you are out at appointments. Tell us your MLS and how many listings you usually carry, and we will send candidates, free of charge, in as soon as 48 hours.

FAQ

Questions about this role

Can a VA enter listings directly into my MLS?
It depends on your MLS. Many allow an assistant login under the agent or broker; others require the agent to submit. Either way, your VA prepares everything and enters it wherever the rules allow.
Will my listing VA write the listing descriptions?
Yes. Your VA drafts the description from your notes, the photos and similar listings, in your voice, and you approve it before it goes live.
Can the same VA handle showing scheduling and feedback?
Yes. Showing coordination, feedback requests and seller updates are a normal part of the role, and usually the part sellers notice most.
What about open houses and listing marketing?
Your VA sets up open house events, prepares flyers and just-listed posts in Canva, and checks that syndication to the portals is correct.
