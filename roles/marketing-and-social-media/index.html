Agents know they should post consistently. Almost none do, because the listing work comes first. A real estate marketing virtual assistant makes consistency automatic: a monthly content calendar, graphics made from your listings and closings, captions written in your voice, and everything scheduled ahead.

We shortlist VAs with a real design eye and a portfolio you can see before you decide. Writing is tested because captions and newsletters go out under your name.

Your VA also handles the pieces that build long-term lead flow: a monthly email to your sphere, Google Business Profile posts and review requests after each closing, and simple edits of the video you shoot on your phone.

At about $7 to $11 an hour, far below an agency retainer or a local marketing hire, a dedicated VA gives you a marketing department that only works on your brand. Tell us your channels and how often you want to post, and we will send candidates within two weeks, free of charge.

FAQ

Questions about this role

Can a marketing VA edit my videos?
Yes. Short-form editing in CapCut or similar, captions, cover frames and scheduling are standard. Long-form production is a different role we can also place.
Will the posts sound like me?
Your VA writes from your voice notes, past posts and a short style guide, and you approve before publishing until you are comfortable letting them run.
Which platforms can they manage?
Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and email newsletters, plus your Google Business Profile.
Can the same VA do listing graphics and newsletters?
Yes. Listing and just-sold graphics, open house promotion, newsletters and review requests are all part of the role.
