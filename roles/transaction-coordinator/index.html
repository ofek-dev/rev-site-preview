Once a contract is signed, the job turns into dates, documents and follow-up. A transaction coordinator virtual assistant handles all of that so you can get back to selling. They open the file, build the timeline, send the disclosures, and follow up with everyone until the deal is clear to close.

We only shortlist coordinators who have run real transactions before and know what an earnest money deadline or an inspection contingency means in practice. We confirm their English on a live interview, because they will be writing to your clients, the other agent, the lender and the title company every day.

Your coordinator works inside your systems and follows your brokerage's checklist, so the file is complete without anyone chasing it. You get a weekly summary of every open deal and what happens next.

At about $7 to $11 an hour with no placement fee, most agents can afford a dedicated coordinator much sooner than they expected. Tell us how many deals you close a month and which systems you use, and we will send candidates, free of charge, in as soon as 48 hours.

Fit

Who this role suits

Agents closing 2 or more deals a month

That is enough paperwork to eat into your selling time. A coordinator gives that time back.

Teams and small brokerages

One coordinator can handle the deals of several agents and keep every file done the same way.

Investors who close often

If you buy or sell regularly, you want the same clean process every time. A dedicated coordinator gives you that.

FAQ

Questions about this role

Does a transaction coordinator VA need a real estate license?
No, not for the admin side. Your coordinator prepares and tracks documents and follows up with everyone involved. Anything that needs a license, like negotiating terms, stays with you. Check your state and brokerage rules for anything specific.
Can my TC VA work directly with clients and the other agent?
Yes, and most do. They send status updates, request signatures and confirm deadlines in your name and your tone, with you copied.
What systems do your coordinators know?
Dotloop, SkySlope, DocuSign and Brokermint are the most common, plus Google Workspace. Tell us what you use and we will match for it.
How many files can one TC VA handle?
A full-time coordinator can comfortably handle a busy team's deals. If you grow, you can add hours or a second coordinator through us with no placement fee.
