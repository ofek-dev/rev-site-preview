Most agents lose deals in the gap between a lead coming in and somebody picking up the phone. An ISA virtual assistant closes that gap. They start the day with your dialer and your lists, speak to every new lead within minutes, and hand you a calendar of qualified appointments instead of a spreadsheet of names.

Every ISA we place has already done the job. We test for a clear phone voice, objection handling and the discipline to make the same calls every day, then verify English fluency on a live video interview. Only the top 1% of applicants make it onto a client shortlist.

You set the scripts, the call windows and the rules. Your ISA logs every conversation in your CRM, tags the outcome, and schedules the follow-up, so nothing goes cold and you can see exactly what the day produced.

Because your ISA is based in the Philippines and works on your schedule, you get a full-time caller at about 20% of what the same seat costs locally. Tell us the lists you want worked and the hours you want covered, and we will send two or three candidates within two weeks.

Fit

Who this role suits

Teams buying leads

If you pay for portal or PPC leads, speed-to-lead decides whether that money converts. A dedicated caller answers first.

Listing-focused agents

Expireds and FSBOs need consistent daily calling. Your ISA makes the calls while you take the listing appointments.

Investors and wholesalers

Motivated-seller lists are a volume game. An ISA works them every day and books the walk-throughs.

FAQ

Questions about this role

Will a Philippines-based ISA sound natural on the phone with US sellers?
Yes. We only shortlist candidates with fluent, neutral spoken English, verified on a live Zoom interview, and most have prior US real estate calling experience. You hear each candidate's intro video before you decide.
Which dialers and CRMs can an ISA VA use?
Mojo, Vulcan7, RedX, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, BoomTown and Lofty are the most common. If you use something else, say so in the brief and we shortlist candidates who already know it.
How are call compliance and do-not-call rules handled?
Your ISA follows your scripts, your call windows and your do-not-call process. You stay in control of the lists and the rules; the VA executes them and logs every call.
How many hours should I start with?
Most clients start with 20 to 40 hours a week scheduled in their local prime calling windows. You can adjust hours with your VA directly as the pipeline grows.
