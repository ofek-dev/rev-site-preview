Most real estate databases are a graveyard of leads that were never followed up. A CRM and lead follow-up virtual assistant turns that database back into a pipeline. They answer new leads by text within minutes, tag and segment every contact, and make sure each one is in a campaign that matches where they are.

We shortlist VAs who already know the main real estate CRMs and can write a text that sounds like a person, not a template. Writing is tested, because tone decides whether a lead replies.

Your VA also owns the unglamorous work that makes a CRM useful: merging duplicates, fixing bad data, setting agent tasks, requesting reviews after closings and pulling a weekly report on response times and lead sources.

At about $7 to $11 an hour with no placement fee, a dedicated follow-up VA pays for itself with a single recovered deal. Tell us your CRM and how many leads you get a month, and we will send candidates within two weeks, free of charge.

FAQ

Questions about this role

How fast can a VA respond to new leads?
During scheduled hours, within minutes. Most clients schedule their VA across the hours when their lead sources are busiest, including evenings and weekends if needed.
Which real estate CRMs do your VAs know?
Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Lofty, BoomTown, Sierra Interactive and Real Geeks are the most common. Name yours in the brief and we shortlist for it.
Can my VA text and email leads as me?
Yes, from your CRM's connected number and email, using your templates and tone. You see every conversation in the CRM.
Will the VA clean up my existing database?
Yes. Deduplication, tagging, fixing missing fields and placing old contacts into the right campaigns is usually the first project.
