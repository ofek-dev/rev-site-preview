Virtual assistant vs. in-house assistant

A side-by-side comparison for agents, teams and property managers deciding how to staff admin, follow-up and marketing.

Short answerFor admin, follow-up, coordination and marketing, a pre-vetted real estate virtual assistant wins for most agents and teams: experienced, full-time help at about 20% of the cost of a local hire, available within two weeks, with a replacement guarantee. An in-house assistant still makes sense when the role needs a physical presence every day, such as running open houses, staging or courier work.

Verdict

If the work happens on a screen or a phone, a real estate virtual assistant is the better hire for most agents, teams and property managers. If the work needs someone physically in your market every day, hire locally for that part and keep a VA for everything else. Many of our clients run exactly that split: a local showing assistant plus a remote ISA, coordinator or marketer.
