Privacy policy

Real Estate Virtual Staff is operated by The Task Trooper, LLC, doing business as Talent Trooper ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use this website, request candidates or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

Information you give us on our forms (name, company, email, phone, the role you want to fill), messages you send us, and standard technical data such as pages visited and device type collected through cookies or similar tools.

How we use it

To process and manage your inquiry, recruit and present candidates, send hiring-process notifications, respond to questions, improve this website, and, if you opt in, send occasional updates about our services. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS terms

If you opt in to text messages, you authorize us to send messages to the number you provided about your request, hiring-process updates and appointment reminders. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies; you may receive up to 10 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Your mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the business (for example our CRM, scheduling and email tools), with candidates to the extent needed to arrange interviews you request, and when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing info@realestatevirtualstaff.com. Browser settings let you control cookies.
