About

Built by founders who hired their own VAs first

Real Estate Virtual Staff is the real estate division of Talent Trooper, a recruiting company that places the top 1% of virtual assistants from the Philippines, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia and more with growing businesses.

Talent Trooper started inside another business. Our founders were running a beverage startup and noticed that half of every day went to high-value work and the other half went to repetitive tasks that still had to be done. After two years of fourteen-hour days, they hired their first virtual assistant from the Philippines.

That one hire became a team of more than sixty full-time VAs, and the founders' hundred-hour weeks became ten-hour weeks. The time that freed up went into a new company: helping other owners find the same leverage, with the screening done for them.

Real estate turned out to be where the leverage is largest. Agents, teams, investors and property managers live on follow-up, paperwork and daily admin, and almost all of it can be done brilliantly from a screen in another time zone. So we built a division that recruits and vets only for those roles.

Why we exist

Anyone can find virtual help online. The hard part is knowing whether they are good. We run every candidate through five or more rounds, present only the top 1%, and stand behind each placement with a replacement guarantee that never expires.

We also handle the parts that make owners nervous about hiring abroad: the agreement, compliance guidance, payment setup and productivity reporting. You get a vetted teammate and a one-page agreement, not a project.

And we keep the money simple. Searching, vetting and meeting candidates costs nothing. When you hire, you pay the VA's hourly rate, typically $7 to $11 depending on experience, and that is the whole bill: no placement fee, no hidden costs.
