FAQ

Real estate virtual assistant questions

Everything agents, teams and property managers ask before their first hire.

What does a real estate virtual assistant do?
A real estate virtual assistant handles the work that does not need a license: cold calling and appointment setting, transaction coordination, listing and MLS input, CRM follow-up, property management admin and marketing. Real Estate Virtual Staff places dedicated VAs for each of those roles.
How much does a real estate virtual assistant cost?
Between $7 and $11 per hour, depending on the candidate's experience, and that hourly rate is all you pay. Real Estate Virtual Staff searches, vets and introduces candidates free of charge, there is no placement fee, and there are no hidden costs. You see each candidate's exact hourly rate on their profile before you decide.
Is there any fee to search for or interview candidates?
No. The search, the vetting and the introductions to two or three candidates are free, with no commitment. You pay only the VA's hourly rate once you hire someone, and nothing if you do not.
Where are your virtual assistants based?
Across the Philippines, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia and other English-fluent markets. Every candidate, wherever they live, clears the same five-plus interview rounds and works the hours you set in your time zone.
Can a real estate VA work in my time zone?
Yes. Every VA we place works the hours you set, in your local time zone. We have placed VAs on Pacific, Mountain, Central and Eastern schedules, including evenings and weekends for lead response.
How are candidates vetted?
We only present the top 1% of applicants. Each candidate goes through five or more rounds: a written test, a live Zoom interview to verify English fluency, a general IQ test, role-specific skills and tools checks, and reference verification. You then see intro videos and resumes before any interview.
How fast can I hire a real estate virtual assistant?
You receive two or three vetted candidates with intro videos, resumes and rates in as soon as 48 hours, and in as soon as 48 hours at most. Most clients interview and choose within a few days after that, and the VA can usually start the following week.
What if the VA is not a good fit?
We replace them at no additional charge, at any point, for as long as you work with us. That includes a VA who leaves. In practice it works like an unlimited trial: you pay the VA for hours worked, and you never pay us again to find a replacement.
Who employs and pays the virtual assistant?
You do. Real Estate Virtual Staff, a Talent Trooper company, recruits, vets and places the VA free of charge, prepares the agreement, and helps you set up compliant payment. You pay the VA's hourly rate only, and the VA works directly for your business like any team member.
Is an overseas VA better than hiring locally?
For admin, follow-up, coordination and marketing, most of our clients say yes: experienced, fluent, full-time help at $7 to $11 an hour instead of a local salary, with a replacement guarantee. Read our in-house vs. virtual assistant comparison for the full breakdown.
Do real estate virtual assistants need a license?
Not for administrative, marketing, coordination or calling work done under your direction. Licensed activities such as negotiating or representing clients stay with you. Rules vary by state and brokerage, so we match the brief to what your office allows.
Which tasks should I delegate to a real estate VA first?
Start with the repeatable work that eats your week: lead follow-up and CRM upkeep, listing input and showing coordination, transaction paperwork, and social posting. Once those run, add calling or marketing projects.
How many hours a week should I hire for?
Most clients start between 20 and 40 hours a week. Lead follow-up and ISA roles work best full-time in your prime hours; transaction and marketing roles are often part-time to start. You adjust hours with your VA directly.
How do I communicate with my virtual assistant?
Any way your team already works. Most clients use Slack or Microsoft Teams plus a shared Google Workspace, with a short daily check-in. Your VA reports in your systems, so you can see the work, not just hear about it.
Can a VA make cold calls for my real estate business?
Yes. Our ISA virtual assistants cold call expireds, FSBOs, circle-prospecting and investor lists every day. They follow your scripts, call windows and do-not-call process, and log every conversation in your CRM.
Can one virtual assistant cover several roles?
A generalist VA can handle follow-up, listing admin and basic marketing for a solo agent. As volume grows, most teams split into a caller, a coordinator and a marketer. We will tell you honestly which setup fits your brief.
Is my client and transaction data safe with a remote VA?
Your VA works inside your own accounts with the permissions you set, signs a confidentiality agreement, and can be removed from access at any time. We recommend role-based logins and a password manager, and we help you set that up.
Do you offer a trial period?
Yes, in effect. Meeting candidates is free and carries no commitment, and the replacement guarantee works as an unlimited trial afterwards: if you are not satisfied at any point, we find you a new candidate at no additional charge. You only ever pay the VA for hours actually worked.
Do you also place virtual assistants outside real estate?
Yes. Real Estate Virtual Staff is a Talent Trooper company. Talent Trooper places executive assistants, bookkeepers, customer support, sales development and more for over 260 organizations. Tell us what you need and we will route it.
